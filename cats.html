Meow! Cats are my favorite animal. Here are three fun facts about cats!

1) Cats have night vision that's five to six times better than a humans!

2) A cat's natural body temperature is around 102 degrees. To help stay warm, they love to lay in the sun all day!

3) Cats can run at up to 30 miles per hour!
