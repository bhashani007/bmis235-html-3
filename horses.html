Horses

Neigh! My second favorite animal are horses! Here are three fun facts about horses.

1) All mustang herds in America actually originated from domesticated horses in the 16th century! Mustangs are not native to North America, but were instead brought from Europe by Spanish explorers.

2) Horses have the biggest eyes of any land mammal.

3) Horses' hooves are made of keratin, just like human hair and nails! If a horse is domesticated, their owner usually has to clip them down.
