Wolves

Awooo! My third favorite animal are wolves! Here are three fun facts about wolves.

1) Wolves are all born with blue eyes, which change to a yellow/orange color as they grow up! 2) Contrary to popular belief, wolves don't actually howl at the moon. This idea came from ancient mythologies around the world. 3) While hunting, each wolf in the pack assumes a different role, making their hunt more successful.
